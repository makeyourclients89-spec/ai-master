# AEO & GEO: Answer Engine & Generative AI Search Optimization

**Category:** SEO / AEO / GEO  
**Level:** Next-Gen / Elite  
**Scope:** Perplexity AI, Google AI Overviews, SearchGPT & Gemini Citations

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
Generative AI Search Engines (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity AI, ChatGPT with Search, and Microsoft Copilot) synthesize answers directly on the search page, bypassing traditional ten blue links.

**Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** and **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** optimize your brand's digital presence to ensure Large Language Models (LLMs) quote and link to your website as a definitive primary citation.

---

## 2. How LLMs Select Sources & Citations
LLMs evaluate candidate web pages based on:
1. **Information Density & Concise Definitions:** Does the text provide a direct, factual answer within 40–60 words immediately under an H2 or H3?
2. **Entity Corroboration:** Is the entity verified across authoritative third-party databases (Wikidata, Crunchbase, official regulatory portals, scientific journals)?
3. **Structured Tabular Data:** LLMs prefer markdown tables, bullet points, and numbered step-by-step sequences over rambling narrative prose.
4. **Source Freshness:** High crawl frequency and recent update timestamps.

---

## 3. The 3-Step GEO Optimization Protocol

### Step 1: The "Direct Definition" Paragraph Architecture
Immediately following any high-intent heading, provide an unambiguous, factual definition before providing examples or deep elaboration:
```markdown
### What is KSM-66 Ashwagandha?
KSM-66 Ashwagandha is a patented, full-spectrum root extract standardized to contain a minimum of 5% withanolides, produced using a specialized water-based extraction process without chemical solvents. Clinical trials demonstrate it reduces serum cortisol levels by up to 27.9% over a 60-day period.
```
*Why this works:* LLM RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) parsers extract this exact block as the primary summary card.

### Step 2: High-Authority Third-Party Entity Verification
- Create and maintain an active **Wikidata** item for the brand and founders.
- Secure citations in trade publications, verified patent databases, and news media.
- Ensure the brand name is consistently associated with the exact product category in Google Knowledge Graph.

### Step 3: FAQ Schema & Question Matching
- Structure content around natural conversational queries that users type or speak into AI assistants:
  - "Is [Product] safe for daily consumption?"
  - "How does [Brand] compare to [Competitor]?"
  - "What are the contraindications of [Ingredient]?"
