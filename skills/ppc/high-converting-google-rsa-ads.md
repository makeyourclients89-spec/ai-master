# Google Ads Responsive Search Ads (RSA) & Quality Score Optimization

**Category:** PPC  
**Level:** Advanced  
**Scope:** Search Campaigns, RSA Headlines, Negative Keywords & ROAS Maximization

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
Responsive Search Ads (RSA) dynamically test combinations of up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions to optimize for conversion intent. To achieve **Excellent Ad Strength** and higher **Quality Scores (8/10+)**, headlines must be structured across specific psychological intent categories.

---

## 2. The 15-Headline RSA Matrix Formula
When building Google Search Ads, balance the 15 headlines into 4 distinct quadrants:
1. **Quadrant 1: Exact Match Keyword Insertion (3 Headlines)**
   - `{KeyWord:Ayurvedic Hair Fall Oil}`
   - `Natural Hair Growth Oil`
   - `Certified Herbal Formulation`
2. **Quadrant 2: Unique Selling Proposition / Value (4 Headlines)**
   - `100% Cold-Pressed Organic Herbs`
   - `Clinically Proven Hair Therapy`
   - `Zero Mineral Oil or Chemicals`
   - `AYUSH Certified & Tested`
3. **Quadrant 3: Social Proof & Trust (4 Headlines)**
   - `Trusted by 50,000+ Customers`
   - `Rated 4.9/5 by Verified Users`
   - `Dermatologically Approved`
   - `100% Satisfaction Guarantee`
4. **Quadrant 4: Strong Direct Call to Action (4 Headlines)**
   - `Shop Online & Get Free Delivery`
   - `Buy 2 Get 1 Free Today Only`
   - `Order Now with Special Discount`
   - `Explore Our Official Range`

---

## 3. Negative Keyword Master Checklist
Eliminate ad spend waste by immediately adding comprehensive negative keyword lists at the campaign level:
- Free / Pirated terms: `free`, `torrent`, `pdf`, `download`, `diy`, `homemade`
- Job seeker terms: `jobs`, `salary`, `careers`, `internship`, `hiring`, `resume`
- Low-intent terms: `meaning`, `wikipedia`, `definition`, `history`, `drawing`
